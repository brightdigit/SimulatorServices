## 1.1.0-beta.4

* **Breaking:** requires Swift 6.4 (`swift-tools-version: 6.4`, Swift 6 language mode, Xcode 27.2 or later) and macOS 15.4 (was macOS 13; needed for `isolated deinit` in the FSEvents watcher). The iOS 16 / Mac Catalyst 16 / watchOS 9 / tvOS 16 / visionOS 1 minimums are unchanged. Stay on 1.1.0-beta.3 if you can't move to Swift 6.4 by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* **Breaking:** typed throws everywhere. `SimCtl.run(_:)` throws `SimCtlError`, and `Subcommand.parse(_:)` / `recover(_:)` are now `throws(SimCtlError)`; update custom `Subcommand` conformances to match and wrap other errors in a `SimCtlError` case. `RuntimeID.init(suffix:)` and `PrefixedDecodableString.init(suffix:)` throw `DecodingError`, and `suffix(forString:)` throws `PrefixMismatchError` by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* **Breaking:** `GetAppContainer.Error` and `List.Error` are removed. Catch `SimCtlError.unreadableOutput` instead of `.missingData` (a simulator without the app's container) or `.deocdingError`, and read `exitCode`, `standardError` and `reason` (`.invalidDevice`, `.invalidState`, `.noMatchingRuntime`, `.other`) for a failed `simctl` by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* **Breaking:** commands run through [swift-subprocess](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift-subprocess) instead of `Process`. The public `Process.run(till:)` extension and `ProcessError.timeout` are removed; `ProcessError` gains `launchFailed` and `unreadableOutput`, and cancelling the calling task terminates `simctl` and throws `SimCtlError.cancelled` by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* **Breaking:** `DeviceState` adds `booting` and `shuttingDown`, and `DeviceTypeID` adds the device types Xcode 27 lists (iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Air, Apple Watch Ultra 4 (49mm), the M5 iPad Pros and more); add the new cases to exhaustive `switch`es by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add `RuntimeList`, `RuntimeDelete`, `DeviceDelete`, `DeleteUnavailable`, `Boot`, `Shutdown` and `Erase` subcommands, and `RuntimeImage` (with `canDelete`, `RuntimeImageKind` and `RuntimeImageState`) by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add `SimCtl(developerDirectory:)` and `SimCtl.find(developerDirectory:)`, which run `simctl` from a chosen Xcode via `DEVELOPER_DIR` (macOS) by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add Xcode discovery and resolution: `XcodeInstallation`, `XcodeSnapshot`, `XcodePreference`, `XcodeResolution` and `XcodeLocating`, with the live `XcodeLocator` (macOS) scanning the application folders, Spotlight and `xcode-select -p`, and streaming new snapshots through FSEvents (or polling) as Xcodes are added, removed or selected by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add the `SimCtlCommands` / `SimCtlDeletionCommands` seam with `LiveSimCtlCommands` (macOS), so apps can test their `simctl` calls against a fake by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add `SimctlSnapshot` (one read of `simctl runtime list -j` and `simctl list -j`), `SimulatorAttribution` (which simulators use which installed runtime) and `DirectorySizeMeasurer` (macOS) by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add version parsing: `VersionNumber`, `BuildNumber`, `MajorMinorVersion` and `VersionedBuild`, compared numerically rather than as strings by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add `Device.lastUsedAt` and `availabilityError`, `Runtime.availabilityError`, `RuntimeID.variant` and `Platform.displayName` (`xrOS` shows as visionOS); the models are `Hashable` and gain public memberwise initializers by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Write value mappings as data: `RawDefined` types are `RawMapped` with a single `BidirectionalMap` `rawOptions` table by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Fix `simctl` failing with `CancellationError` under `xcodebuild test`: the variables xcodebuild injects (`DYLD_*`, `__XPC_DYLD_*`, `XCTest*`, `XCInjectBundle*`) are no longer passed to child processes by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Linux and the non-macOS Apple platforms build the subcommands, models and version parsing; running `simctl`, `xcode-select` and `mdfind` and watching for Xcodes stay macOS-only, and the Subprocess dependency is conditioned to macOS by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Tests: `simctl` and Xcode fixtures recorded from Xcode 27.1 and 27.2; the test bundle builds and signs on iOS, watchOS, tvOS and visionOS; macOS-only suites take `.enabled(if: Platform.isMacOS)` from a new `Platform` test helper (alongside their `#if os(macOS)`), and always-true `#available(iOS 13.4, *)` checks are removed by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* CI: Swift 6.4 Linux lanes (`swift:6.4-noble`, plus `jammy` for PRs into `main` or a semver branch), Apple lanes on the `xcode-27` image with Xcode 27.2, CodeQL, unsafe-flags and cache-cleanup workflows, `pull_request` triggers only; lint tools move from Mint to mise (swift-format 604, SwiftLint 0.65.1, periphery); Codecov and CodeFactor are dropped by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Add `Scripts/docc.sh` DocC builds (no swift-docc-plugin dependency), a Makefile, a devcontainer, Swift Package Index docs (`.spi.yml`) and agent files by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/29
* Dependencies: add swift-subprocess `from: "1.0.2"` (macOS only); OSVer stays `from: "1.0.0"`
* Add the Claude Code GitHub workflow by @leogdion in https://github.com/brightdigit/SimulatorServices/pull/30
